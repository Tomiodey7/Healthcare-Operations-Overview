Project Analyzing 55,074 admissions, $1.42B revenue and Average Length of Stay 15.51 days

# Business Questions?
1. Are hospital Admissions Increasing or Decreasing?
2. Which Medical Conditions have the Longest Dyays?
3. Which insurance providers generate the highest total billing?
4. Does Admission type affect length of stay and billing?

Tools: Tableau Public, Python(Pandas)

Insights:
- Volume Peaked in 2020 at 11,202 admissions then became stable
- Length of Stay uniform across all medical condions(<2% difference)
- Revenue balanced across 5 insurance providers
